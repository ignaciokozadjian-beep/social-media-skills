# KiKo Social Media

## Overview

Orchestrates social content as a commercial system, not isolated posts. Every piece must connect audience reality, useful expertise, a clear next action, and the account's approved identity.

## Required context

Before producing content, read:

1. `references/nacho-brand-context.md` for @nachoecommerce.
2. `references/content-funnel.md` for commercial intent.
3. `references/visual-system.md` when designing or briefing visuals.
4. `references/editorial-workflow.md` for approval and publishing gates.
5. `references/third-party-notices.md` for adapted methodology sources.

For a KiKo client account, create a separate profile based on the same structure. Never reuse Nacho-specific identity, claims, vocabulary, or visual rules for clients.

## Core principle

Use this sequence:

**real audience situation → empathy → useful insight → evidence or experience → practical next step → appropriate CTA**

Do not start by listing skills, tools, or services. First make the intended reader recognize their own problem, frustration, or ambition.

## Content workflow

1. **Objective** — choose one: reach, authority, engagement, lead capture, qualification, or sale.
2. **Audience moment** — define who should identify with the content and what they are experiencing.
3. **Single promise** — one useful takeaway only.
4. **Evidence** — experience, process, example, verified result, or clearly labeled opinion.
5. **Format** — carousel, Reel, static post, story, resource, or web article.
6. **Script** — hook, development, payoff, CTA.
7. **Approval gate** — present the slide-by-slide or scene-by-scene brief before generating final assets.
8. **Design** — apply the profile's visual system exactly.
9. **Caption** — support the visual; do not merely repeat it.
10. **Distribution** — define cover, alt text, tags, posting notes, and whether to pin.
11. **Measurement** — record reach, non-follower reach, profile visits, follows, saves, shares, link clicks, leads, and sales where available.
12. **Learning** — record what topic, hook, problem, proof, format, and CTA performed.

## CTA rules

Choose the CTA from the content objective. Do not add every CTA automatically.

- **Follow** — presentation, positioning, recurring series, account promise.
- **Like** — identity, opinion, or introduction where low-friction support is appropriate.
- **Save** — checklists, procedures, frameworks, reference content.
- **Share** — only when the content solves a recognizable problem for another person.
- **Link in bio** — resources, waitlists, diagnostics, or services.
- **Comment or DM** — when conversation or qualification is the goal.

For Nacho, never use the expressions “sin humo” or “sin vueltas”.

## Carousel rules

- Vertical 4:5, 1080 × 1350.
- Cover communicates one clear promise.
- One main idea per slide.
- Keep text mobile-readable; shorten before reducing font size.
- The final slide must create emotional relevance and a next action.
- Present the complete brief for approval before final image generation.
- Do not expose confidential client or employer information.

## Regional relevance

For Uruguay and Latin America:

- Use natural Rioplatense Spanish unless another market is specified.
- Prefer situations common to small and medium ecommerce teams: manual work, fragmented systems, limited staff, logistics, stock, conversion, marketplaces, and unclear data.
- Avoid imported jargon when a clear regional phrase exists.
- Do not claim that an approach is universally valid across Latin America without evidence.

## Commercial alignment

Every content series should lead toward one of these paths:

- Content → follow.
- Content → free resource.
- Free resource → owned contact.
- Contact → diagnostic conversation.
- Diagnostic → paid implementation or recurring service.
- Repeated solution → scalable skill, template, automation, or software.

Educational value must remain complete and honest. Do not manufacture artificial gaps solely to force a sale.

## Approval and safety

- Never publish, schedule, message, modify a profile, or change a live website without explicit approval.
- Never invent metrics, clients, results, testimonials, or experience.
- Separate verified fact, professional opinion, and proposed hypothesis.
- Recheck current platform rules before relying on changing algorithm, hashtag, advertising, or feature guidance.

## Quality checklist

Before delivery, verify:

- Does the audience recognize a real situation?
- Is the content useful without purchasing anything?
- Is the claim supported or clearly framed as opinion?
- Is there one main idea?
- Does the CTA match the objective?
- Is the design consistent with the profile?
- Is the language natural for the intended market?
- Does it avoid confidential information and prohibited phrases?
- Is the next commercial step relevant rather than forced?
