# Nacho Ecommerce Content Funnel

## Business model

The brand grows through useful public content and free resources, captures relevant demand on an owned website, and monetizes through paid ecommerce services. Repeated solutions can later become scalable products.

## Funnel

### 1. Discovery

Channels:

- Instagram feed.
- Reels.
- Stories.
- Searchable web content.
- Collaborations and referrals.

Content goals:

- Make a real problem recognizable.
- Demonstrate practical expertise.
- Earn a profile visit or follow.

### 2. Trust

Assets:

- Educational carousels.
- Practical breakdowns.
- Real or anonymized cases.
- Behind-the-scenes process explanations.
- Clear opinions with evidence.

Trust signals:

- Specificity.
- Regional relevance.
- Honest limitations.
- Repeatable methodology.
- Verified professional experience.

### 3. Lead magnet

Initial priority:

- One high-value free resource tied directly to the paid service.

Recommended first resource:

- Shopify automation checklist or diagnostic map.

Resource principles:

- Solve one defined problem.
- Deliver immediate value.
- Help the reader diagnose their situation.
- Introduce a logical next step without withholding essential information.

### 4. Owned audience

The website should collect only information required for the next step.

Minimum fields:

- Name.
- Email.

Optional qualification fields:

- Store platform.
- Business stage.
- Main operational problem.

After registration:

1. Deliver the resource.
2. Show a relevant thank-you page.
3. Offer a diagnostic or related case.
4. Send a short educational follow-up sequence.
5. Invite a conversation only when relevant.

### 5. Paid entry offer

Recommended options:

- Shopify diagnostic.
- Automation opportunity map.
- Conversion and operations audit.
- Process review.

The entry offer should be specific, fixed in scope, and produce a tangible output.

### 6. Core services

- Shopify optimization and implementation.
- Ecommerce automation.
- Practical AI implementation.
- Dashboards and operational tools.
- Custom ecommerce solutions.
- Recurring Ecommerce Manager support.

### 7. Scalable products

Create only after observing repeated demand:

- Skills.
- Templates.
- Automation packages.
- Dashboards.
- Microsoftware.
- Resource library or membership.

## Content-to-offer mapping

| Content topic | Natural free resource | Natural paid service |
|---|---|---|
| Manual ecommerce tasks | Automation checklist | Automation map or implementation |
| Shopify conversion problem | Mobile/PDP checklist | CRO audit or optimization sprint |
| Fragmented data | Reporting template | Dashboard implementation |
| AI uncertainty | Practical AI guide | AI workflow implementation |
| Operational disorder | Process documentation template | Operations review or recurring support |

## Measurement

Do not evaluate the strategy only by follower count.

Track:

- Non-follower reach.
- Profile visits.
- Follows per post.
- Link clicks.
- Resource conversion rate.
- Qualified contacts.
- Diagnostic requests.
- Proposals.
- Sales and revenue by source.

Primary business metric:

**Qualified commercial opportunities generated by content.**
