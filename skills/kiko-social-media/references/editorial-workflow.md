# KiKo Editorial Workflow

## Purpose

Ensure every post is strategically useful, visually consistent, approved before production, and measured after publication.

## Stage 1: Brief

Define:

- Account or profile.
- Business objective.
- Audience segment.
- Real audience situation.
- One core message.
- Evidence available.
- Desired next action.
- Platform and format.

Do not draft until the objective and audience situation are clear.

## Stage 2: Content architecture

For a carousel, provide:

- Slide number.
- Purpose.
- Headline.
- Supporting copy.
- Visual direction.
- Transition to the next slide.

For a Reel, provide:

- Opening hook.
- Scene sequence.
- Spoken or on-screen copy.
- Proof or demonstration.
- Closing CTA.

For static content, provide:

- Visual headline.
- Supporting idea.
- Caption role.
- CTA.

## Stage 3: Empathy review

Verify:

- The reader can recognize their own reality.
- The content does not assume unlimited budget, staff, or technical maturity.
- The language does not sound imported or detached from Uruguay and Latin America.
- The post offers practical help rather than only displaying expertise.

## Stage 4: Claim review

Classify every material claim as:

- Verified fact.
- Personal or professional experience.
- Inference.
- Opinion.
- Hypothesis to test.

Remove or qualify unsupported claims. Never invent performance numbers or testimonials.

## Stage 5: Brief approval gate

Present the complete brief to Ignacio before generating final design assets.

The approval must cover:

- Message.
- Slide or scene order.
- CTA.
- Visual route.
- Any use of personal images, client references, or real cases.

Do not proceed to final asset production until explicitly approved.

## Stage 6: Production

Apply the approved profile and visual system.

Production checks:

- 1080 × 1350 for vertical feed posts.
- Mobile-readable typography.
- Consistent palette and hierarchy.
- No accidental logos or unapproved marks.
- No visible spelling errors in decorative code.
- No confidential information.

## Stage 7: Caption package

Deliver:

- Final caption.
- First-line hook.
- CTA.
- Suggested hashtags only when useful.
- Alt text.
- Tagging or location recommendations.
- Posting order and cover selection.
- Pinning recommendation.

Hashtags are not mandatory. Prefer a few specific tags over generic blocks, and verify current platform guidance when strategy depends on them.

## Stage 8: Publishing approval

Never publish, schedule, edit the profile, or message users without explicit approval.

## Stage 9: Performance review

Record after a useful measurement window:

- Reach.
- Non-follower reach.
- Impressions.
- Likes.
- Comments.
- Saves.
- Shares.
- Profile visits.
- Follows.
- Link clicks.
- Leads or conversations.

Calculate where possible:

- Follow conversion from profile visits.
- Profile visit rate from reach.
- Save and share rate.
- Link click rate.
- Lead conversion from website visits.

## Stage 10: Learning log

For each post, record:

- Topic.
- Audience problem.
- Hook type.
- Format.
- Proof type.
- CTA.
- Result.
- What to repeat.
- What to change.

Use actual data to update future recommendations. Do not generalize from a single post unless the signal is unusually strong and clearly labeled as preliminary.
