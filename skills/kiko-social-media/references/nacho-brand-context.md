## Strategic objective

Build an audience through useful free content and resources, move relevant traffic to an owned website, capture contacts, establish professional authority, and monetize through paid ecommerce services. Over time, convert repeatable solutions into scalable skills, templates, automations, and software.

## Primary audience

- Owners and managers of online stores in Uruguay and Latin America.
- Ecommerce Managers and small operational teams.
- Brands using Shopify or evaluating it.
- Businesses with repetitive manual processes, fragmented information, conversion problems, or limited technical capacity.

## Audience reality

The audience often has to operate with small teams, limited time, inconsistent processes, several sales channels, stock and logistics complexity, and pressure to produce results without a large technology department.

Content must acknowledge that reality. Avoid presenting sophisticated systems as effortless or implying that every business has the same resources.

## Audience goals

- Sell more without losing operational control.
- Reduce repetitive work and errors.
- Improve the Shopify experience and conversion.
- Apply AI in practical, responsible ways.
- Build systems that can grow without multiplying manual tasks.

## Voice

- Clear, precise, professional, and approachable.
- Empathetic without becoming sentimental.
- Technical when useful, translated into business impact.
- Rioplatense Spanish, understandable across Latin America.
- Based on concrete situations, real processes, and applicable lessons.

## Voice rules

- Lead with the audience's situation before speaking about personal expertise.
- Share experience without exaggeration or self-celebration.
- Explain what changed, why it matters, and what someone can do next.
- Use short paragraphs and natural phrasing.
- Avoid excessive emojis, forced informality, generic motivational language, and engagement bait.

## Prohibited expressions

Never use:

- “sin humo”
- “sin vueltas”

Avoid:

- “gurú” positioning.
- Claims of guaranteed growth.
- Empty phrases such as “revolucionar tu negocio”.
- Repetitive lists of Shopify, IA, and automation without a human or commercial context.

## Content pillars

1. **Shopify as a business system** — conversion, UX, performance, catalog, operations, and integrations.
2. **Automation of ecommerce work** — reducing repetition, errors, and dependency on manual control.
3. **Practical AI** — concrete use cases, safeguards, limitations, and workflows.
4. **Real ecommerce operations** — stock, logistics, marketplaces, customer experience, reporting, and regional constraints.
5. **Building solutions** — cases, experiments, tools, resources, skills, dashboards, and lessons from implementation.

## Empathy standard

Every publication should answer at least one:

- What is the reader currently struggling with?
- What are they afraid may fail?
- What repetitive work is consuming their time?
- What outcome are they trying to reach?
- What constraint makes the obvious advice unrealistic?

## Evidence and confidentiality

- Use only verified personal experience and results.
- Do not publish confidential Meller information, internal screenshots, unpublished metrics, credentials, client data, or proprietary processes without explicit authorization.
- Anonymize cases when required.
- Distinguish between real cases, composites, hypothetical examples, and opinions.

## CTA preference

Use one primary CTA aligned with the post:

- Presentation and positioning: follow, optionally like.
- Educational reference: save.
- Relatable operational problem: share with a colleague only when natural.
- Resource: link in bio.
- Commercial qualification: request a diagnostic or send a message.

Do not show “guardalo” or “compartilo” by default on every visual.
