# Nacho Ecommerce Visual System

## Core direction

Dark, editorial, technological, minimal, and credible. The identity should look like an experienced ecommerce operator using technology, not a generic AI creator or gaming brand.

## Palette

- Predominant black and near-black backgrounds.
- Deep burgundy or dark red-orange atmospheric accent.
- Orange-red highlights with restrained saturation.
- Main text in warm white or ivory.
- Avoid bright red glows, neon effects, oversaturated gradients, and large luminous halos.

Exact colour values may be finalized later. Until approved, maintain relative relationships rather than treating temporary samples as permanent brand standards.

## Typography

- Strong condensed or editorial display type for short headlines.
- Clean geometric sans-serif for supporting text.
- Optional monospaced type for subtle programming fragments.
- Prioritize mobile legibility over decorative type.

## Composition

- Vertical 4:5, 1080 × 1350.
- Clear hierarchy and generous negative space.
- One dominant message per slide.
- Portrait may appear on cover, presentation, or case-context slides, but not on every slide.
- CTA slides should often work without a portrait.

## Technology elements

Use sparingly:

- Short, meaningful programming fragments.
- Terminal prompts, brackets, variables, or function-like labels.
- Fine connector lines or nodes.
- Small grids, cursor blocks, or interface marks.

Programming elements must reinforce the message and remain secondary. Never fill the background with decorative code that competes with the content.

## Portrait treatment

- Black shirt preferred.
- Dark burgundy or red-orange background.
- Natural facial proportions and recognizable likeness.
- Controlled rim lighting.
- Avoid excessive skin smoothing, painterly artifacts, hyper-sharpening, or unrealistic eye and hair detail.

## CTA treatment

- Use plain-language CTA with clear visual priority.
- Avoid a permanent row containing every action.
- Select only the action relevant to the post.
- Presentation posts may close with “Seguime” and optionally “Dale like”.
- Resource posts may use “Guardalo” or “Link en bio”.
- Sharing should be requested only when the content naturally helps another person.

## Anti-patterns

Do not use:

- Generic robots, glowing brains, holograms, or stock AI imagery.
- Excessive icons.
- Bright red backgrounds.
- Dense dashboards as decoration.
- Logos or monograms not formally approved.
- Fake code with visible spelling errors.
- Small body text that cannot be read on a phone.
- Different visual identities between slides in the same carousel.
